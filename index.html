HOW TO PLAY

EXPLORATION
Drag move · Double-tap ground dash there (fast scouting) · Two-finger drag pan/tilt camera · Pinch zoom
COMBAT
Tap fire / hurl a bar / shoot a drone · Hold ground wave, Tesla coil on a bar, shatter a drone · Double-tap a drone knock it back · Tap+hold early shield (+50% speed, shatters drones on contact)
MAP OBJECTS
Canisters shoot from range (dark ones = huge) · Green orb +50% health · Blue orb reflects enemy shots
RANKS
Raiders (basic) · Juggernauts (armored) · Marksmen (ranged) · Warlords (elite, drops a pickup)
TACTICAL — bottom left
Gravity Well crushing funnel, opposite quadrant
Ground Spikes spike ring around you, sets off nearby canisters/bars too
Bullet Time toggle — world slows, you don't, your shots freeze until you toggle off
ULTIMATES — bottom right
Omega expanding kill rings · Leveller vortex above you · Overload chain lightning · Overheat melts every drone
MENU (top right ☰)
Pause · instructions · volume · theme · quit to menu
START SCREEN: DIFFICULTY & CHEATS
Difficulty tunes drone strength & your recharge speed. Nukem all canisters huge · Fast Charge 5s cooldowns · Randomiser Well/Spikes fire randomly · Shield Dash shield press = spiral dash · Bulletproof permanent shot-reflect, blue orbs stop spawning
